1. WHAT IS BIDMAGNET?
---------------------
Every year Spanish public bodies (councils, ministries, hospitals,
universities...) buy billions of euros of IT services through public
tenders ("licitaciones"). Small and mid-sized IT companies often miss
out because:
  - tenders are scattered and found too late,
  - it's hard to tell which ones are worth bidding for,
  - the paperwork is long and one missing document gets a bid thrown out,
  - nobody knows what price will win.

BidMagnet is a "virtual bid team" for these companies. A company fills in
its profile once. BidMagnet then shows the tenders that fit it, explains
why, and for the ones it chooses, prepares the groundwork: a checklist of
every required document (each pointing to the exact page of the official
papers), how the bid is scored, and what price is realistic.
The company still submits on the official government platform itself.


2. HOW IT WORKS (in plain words)
--------------------------------
  1. COLLECT    Every day we download all new public tenders from the
                national platform (PLACSP) and keep the IT ones.
  2. UNDERSTAND We read the key facts (budget, deadline, buyer, who won
                before) and, for chosen tenders, the 100+ page documents,
                turning them into a checklist with page references.
  3. MATCH      For each company we score every open tender from 0 to 100
                and list the reasons ("in your region", "needs a
                certificate you don't have", "only 5 days left").
  4. PREPARE    Checklist, scoring rules, price formula, warnings, and
                history of the buyer and competitors.

  Example: a 25-person software firm in Valencia opens BidMagnet and sees
  "85/100 – Cemetery management software, La Vall d'Uixó, 3,704 EUR,
  18 days left. Warning: needs ENS security certification."

4. KEY NUMBERS (January–September 2026)
---------------------------------------
  - 178,645 public tenders published; 16,134 are IT; 424 open right now.
  - 3,882 different companies won IT contracts; the biggest are
    Telefónica, Inetum, Indra, Orange and Vodafone.
  - 4,588 IT contracts are expected to end in the next 12 months
    (chances to replace the current supplier).
  - Usually only 2 companies bid for an IT contract (median).
  - In competitive tenders, winners bid about 10–14% below the budget.
  - 90% of awards show who won; 99% show how many bids came in.
  - Database size: see the storage note in section 7.


5. THE THREE DEMO TENDERS
-------------------------
  - Cemetery software, La Vall d'Uixó (3.7k EUR): easy entry, but its two
    official documents contradict each other on the security level.
  - AI chatbot, Canary Islands Government (50.7k EUR): price barely
    matters, and the requirements look written for one existing supplier.
  - Cybersecurity, Aguas de Lorca (142k EUR, 3 lots): heavy certification
    requirements; bidding for all 3 lots gets you excluded.
  All 116 page references were checked against the original PDFs.

7. STORAGE NOTE (Supabase free tier = 500 MB)
---------------------------------------------
  Before: 154 MB for 4 months (8,688 IT tenders).
  Now:    128 MB for 9 months (16,134 IT tenders), about 26% of the limit.
  How: removed a duplicate copy of every tender, stopped rewriting tenders
  that didn't change, and cleaned up space left by earlier reloads.
  Each tender now takes ~8 KB instead of ~18 KB. Room for roughly 2 more
  years of IT tenders before the free tier is full. The PDFs themselves
  are NOT stored yet (only their links); that would use Supabase Storage
  (1 GB free), which is separate.
